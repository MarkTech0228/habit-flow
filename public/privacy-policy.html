Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 10, 2026

TL;DR: We respect your privacy. Your data is yours, stored securely on Firebase, and never sold to third parties. You can delete your data anytime.

1. Introduction

Welcome to HabitFlow ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address: Used for account creation and authentication
  • Display name: Optional, used for personalization
  • Profile photo: Optional, for visual identification

2.2 User-Generated Content

  • Habits: Your habit tracking data, completion dates, streaks
  • To-do items: Your task lists and completion status
  • Financial data: Expenses, income, budgets, goals (stored locally and in encrypted Firebase)
  • Receipt images: Photos you upload for expense tracking (stored in Firebase Storage)

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information: Device type, operating system, browser type
  • Usage data: Feature usage, app interactions, error logs
  • Analytics data: Firebase Analytics collects anonymized usage patterns

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the HabitFlow service
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Personalize your experience
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our service
  • Send you important service notifications
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where Your Data is Stored

  • Firebase Firestore: Your habits, todos, and financial data
  • Firebase Storage: Your receipt images
  • Local Storage: App settings and cached data on your device

4.2 Security Measures

  • Data encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL
  • Firebase Authentication for secure access
  • Firestore Security Rules prevent unauthorized access
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We DO NOT Sell Your Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5.2 Third-Party Services We Use

  • Firebase (Google): Backend infrastructure, authentication, storage
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Sentry (Optional): Error monitoring and crash reporting
    Sentry Privacy Policy

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights and safety.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access Your Data

You can view all your data within the app at any time.

6.2 Export Your Data

You can export your data in JSON/CSV format from Settings → Export Data.

6.3 Delete Your Data

You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

6.4 Opt-Out of Analytics

You can disable analytics tracking from Settings → Privacy → Analytics.

7. Children's Privacy

HabitFlow is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence, including the United States (where Firebase servers are located). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

9. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained indefinitely while your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Backups: May persist in backups for up to 90 days after deletion

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of HabitFlow after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your data
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time

12. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under CCPA:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights
